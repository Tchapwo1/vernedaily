### Transforming High-Value B2B Prospecting into an Evidence-Led Discipline
**Proposal Version:** 1.0.0  
**Engagement:** 30-Day Paid Commercial Pilot  
**Target Organization:** Founder-Led B2B SaaS & Enterprise Outbound Teams (£15k–£100k+ ACV)  
**Proposed Test Investment:** £1,500 flat (30-day engagement)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Commercial Purpose

Most sales teams do not suffer from a lack of leads; they suffer from a lack of **clarity on which accounts to pursue, why now, and what to say**.

Reps spend over 60% of their working hours manually scrolling LinkedIn and job boards, guessing at tech stacks, and sending generic, templated cold emails with sub-1% reply rates. Meanwhile, Account Executives waste valuable demo calendars on unqualified prospects who lack budget, authority, or genuine operational pain.

The **Strategic Account Intelligence Sprint** is a 30-day, low-friction commercial pilot designed to answer three critical questions for revenue leaders:
1. **Which named accounts in your target territory are genuinely worth pursuing today?**
2. **What primary observable evidence indicates they have an active reason to buy now?**
3. **What is the exact diagnostic conversation opener that turns that evidence into a qualified AE meeting?**

## 3. What Your Team Receives (Pilot Deliverables)

During the 30-day sprint, the engine acts as an analytical force multiplier for your sales motion:

### 1. The Daily Action Queue (10–20 Accounts/Day)
No more staring at a static spreadsheet of 200 names. Reps log in to a curated, ranked queue of the 10–20 accounts most worth acting on today, with the transparent ranking rationale ($H_{\text{priority}}$) and specific next action clearly displayed.

### 2. Audited Evidence Dossiers (Facts vs. Inferences)
Every recommended account includes an auditable intelligence dossier:
* Primary source URLs, publication dates, and exact verbatim quotes (job postings, SEC filings, engineering blogs).
* Clear distinction between **verified facts** and **working inferences**.
* Automatic flagging of stale signals (45–180d half-lives) and cross-source contradictions.

### 3. Signal-Specific Diagnostic Outreach Plans
* Mobile-optimized diagnostic email drafts (**strictly under 75 words**) framed around observable technical trade-offs.
* 15-second cold-calling battlecards with pattern-interrupt openers and objection pivots.
* **Draft-Only Air Gap**: The engine generates reviewable drafts; your reps retain full control and click send.

### 4. The AE Pre-Meeting Discovery Brief
When a meeting is scheduled, the Account Executive receives a 1-page briefing before the call:
* Verified tech stack and operational friction citations.
* Progressive qualification status (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeframe).
* The **recommended first diagnostic question** to open the call with peer-level credibility.
* Specific traps and unverified claims the rep should **avoid** making.

### 5. Final Baseline-vs-Pilot ROI Report
An executive audit comparing sales research time, evidence accuracy, prospect engagement, and AE qualification rates against your pre-pilot baseline.

## 6. Pre-Agreed Success Criteria & Definition of Done

Before launching the pilot, we mutually agree on four measurable performance gates:

| Value Driver | Target Metric | Measurement Methodology |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Sales Capacity Multiplication** | **$\ge 40\%$ reduction** in median account research time | Timed trial comparing the first 15 manual accounts against 15 engine-assisted accounts. |
| **2. Evidence Integrity & Trust** | **$\ge 95\%$ accuracy** on audited material claims | Human AE spot-check of 50 randomized claims against live public sources. |
| **3. Workflow Adoption** | **Repeated daily usage** by participating sellers | Verification that the daily action queue is reviewed, edited, and actioned weekly. |
| **4. Pipeline Quality** | **Directional tracking** of meetings held & AE acceptance | Audited log of held discovery calls, show rates, and conversion to Stage 2 pipeline. |

*Note*: We do not make reckless promises of "guaranteed meetings" or "guaranteed domain safety." We measure concrete efficiency gains, decision quality, and real-world pipeline contributions against your historical baseline.
