# Account planning and discovery preparation

## Account plan format

For Tier 1 and selected Tier 2 accounts, prepare:
1. Account objective and proposed tier
2. Why the account fits
3. Strongest trigger and evidence date
4. Pain hypothesis and product capability fit
5. Current relationship, owner, opportunity, and outreach status
6. Stakeholder map: known roles, likely roles, gaps
7. Account-level narrative and role-specific angles
8. Recommended sequence and dependencies
9. Risks, unknowns, disqualifiers, and coordination needs
10. Success criteria and next review point

Do not produce a lengthy account plan for every Tier 3 record unless requested.

## Discovery preparation

Include:
- Who is attending and verified roles
- What triggered the meeting and what each person has said
- Meeting objective and desired next step
- Relevant account context and source links
- 5–8 questions selected from current state, pain, impact, desired outcome, technical fit, buying process, and timeline
- Likely objections with short, honest responses
- Stakeholder and qualification gaps

## Question ladder

- Current workflow: "How do you handle [workflow] today?"
- Trigger: "What prompted this to become a priority now?"
- Pain: "Where does the current approach fall short?"
- Impact: "How does that affect the team or business?"
- Desired outcome: "What would success look like, and how would you measure it?"
- Technical fit: "Which systems, data, security, or rollout requirements matter?"
- Buying process: "Who else should help evaluate this, and how are decisions made?"
- Timing: "Is there a target date or dependency we should understand?"

Choose questions appropriate to the person. Do not assume every stakeholder knows budget or procurement details.

## Meeting booking

If calendar access is connected and authorized:
- Confirm timezone and duration.
- Check real availability.
- Offer 2–3 options only when verified.
- Do not create/send invites without explicit authorization.
- Never claim availability based on guesswork.
