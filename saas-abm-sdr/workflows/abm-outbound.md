# Workflow: ABM outbound prospecting

1. **Understand the campaign.** Reuse known product, ICP, segment, objective, tiers, CTA, approved proof, exclusions, cadence, and campaign window. Ask only for missing essentials. If absent, offer provisional tiering rather than inventing criteria.
2. **Discover tools and permissions.** Identify available web, CRM, Gmail, calendar, and sales engagement actions. Read schemas. Do not assume access.
3. **Normalize the account universe.** Deduplicate by CRM ID or verified domain; flag ambiguous parent/subsidiary/name matches.
4. **Check eligibility.** Where authorized, inspect account owner, customer status, open opportunities, recent activity, active sequences, duplicates, opt-outs, and suppression.
5. **Screen and tier accounts.** Apply account-selection criteria and scoring. Keep unknowns and evidence coverage visible. Do not spend deep research effort equally across all accounts.
6. **Research prioritized accounts.** Verify firmographic/use-case fit, strongest trigger, relevant initiatives, and evidence dates. Record sources and confidence.
7. **Map stakeholders.** Identify a small set of relevant people for each account. Verify current role and professional relevance. Mark buying roles as verified, likely, or unknown.
8. **Build a shared account narrative.** State why the account fits, why now (if a valid trigger exists), the pain hypothesis, product capability, proof, CTA, and unknowns.
9. **Coordinate messages.** Assign differentiated angles and timing to contacts. Avoid duplicate or contradictory outreach. Define dependencies and stop conditions.
10. **Draft and QA.** Apply outreach and compliance references. Show proposed subject/body in chat.
11. **Create drafts if requested/authorized.** Default to first touch only unless all drafts are explicitly requested. Check for duplicate drafts and verify each result.
12. **Report status.** Summarize tier, account rationale, committee coverage, messages, sources, actions completed/failed/skipped, and unresolved checks.

## Stop conditions

Do not create outreach drafts for a known opt-out, hard bounce, disqualified account, unresolved identity, unauthorized recipient, or unresolved account-owner/opportunity conflict where policy requires coordination. Do not send messages.
