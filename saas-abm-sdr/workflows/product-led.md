# Workflow: product-led signals within ABM

Use only when authorized first-party product analytics are available and the user's business has a product-led motion.

1. Confirm event definitions, observation window, and thresholds from documented product analytics or user instructions. Never invent thresholds.
2. Associate events to a person and account only when the identity mapping is reliable and permitted.
3. Distinguish individual activity from account-level engagement. Do not imply all stakeholders are active because one user is active.
4. Score account fit separately from product intent. High usage at a poor-fit account is not automatically a qualified opportunity.
5. Check account owner, customer status, open opportunity, suppression, and recent outreach.
6. Recommend a helpful next step appropriate to the signal: enablement, onboarding, qualification, or sales conversation.
7. Draft only when authorized; never send. Explain which verified signal and time window informed the recommendation.
8. If product data or identity mapping is unavailable, state the limitation and do not invent a PQL signal.
