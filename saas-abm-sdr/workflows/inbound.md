# Workflow: inbound lead within an account-based motion

1. Record the inbound event, person, account, source, and timestamp from available authorized data.
2. Verify account association; do not merge a person into an account based only on a common name.
3. Check owner, customer/opportunity status, recent activity, duplicate records, opt-out, and routing rules.
4. Score account fit separately from the person's intent. A high-intent request from a poor-fit account still requires fit review; a strong-fit account is not automatically high intent.
5. Route to the account owner or relevant team if an account already has an owner or active opportunity.
6. Recommend the next action according to the user's SLA and process. Do not invent an SLA.
7. Draft a response only if requested/authorized; never send automatically.
8. Report account, contact, event evidence, score/coverage, routing, unknowns, and actual action status.
