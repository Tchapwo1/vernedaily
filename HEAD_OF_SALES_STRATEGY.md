## 2. The Initial Wedge: Founder-Led B2B SaaS (£15k–£100k+ ACV)

We deliberately reject serving every generic outbound team. We focus strictly on:

### The Target Customer Profile (Initial ICP):
* **Company Type**: B2B SaaS with an expensive, non-trivial business problem (infrastructure, cybersecurity, data, fintech, compliance).
* **Deal Size**: Annual Contract Value (ACV) between **£15,000 and £100,000+**.
* **Primary Buyer**: Founder, CEO, or Commercial Co-Founder personally responsible for revenue generation.
* **Sales Motion**: High-value account-based selling: researching named accounts, identifying key stakeholders, developing a point of view, and multi-touch pursuit.
* **The Acute Pain**: The founder has strictly limited selling hours (5–10 hrs/week), too many potential accounts, zero visibility into real buying triggers, and no structured process to turn research into high-conviction discovery calls.

### Who We Explicitly Reject Initially:
1. **Pre-ICP Startups**: Early founders without a validated product or repeatable customer profile.
2. **Low-Value Products (<£10k ACV)**: Cannot economically justify high-touch account intelligence.
3. **Large Enterprises**: Too much procurement friction, security review red tape, and custom CRM integration requirements.
4. **Outbound Agencies**: Addicted to mass-volume email blasts across disparate client niches.

## 3. The 6 Core Workflow Changes

| # | Workflow Change | Why It Matters | Commercial Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Daily Action Queue (10–20 accounts)** | Replaces the static 200-account spreadsheet. Reps receive the top 10–20 accounts most worth acting on today with an explicit ranking rationale and recommended next action. | Eliminates decision fatigue; establishes a disciplined daily sales routine. |
| **2** | **Visible Evidence Quality** | Separates primary verified facts from working inferences. Exposes source URLs, dates, and exact quotes. Contradictory or stale signals trigger review. | Defensibility, buyer trust, and elimination of embarrassing outreach mistakes. |
| **3** | **Decoupled Fit vs. Momentum** | A perfect ICP account with zero friction triggers does not jump ahead of a high-fit account undergoing an active migration. Both dimensions are visible. | Reps understand *why* an account is prioritized and can challenge recommendations. |
| **4** | **Signal-Specific Outreach** | Explains the observed trigger, why it matters to this stakeholder, what remains uncertain, and the best diagnostic question. Draft copy (<75 words) is generated only after these steps. | Relevant executive conversations rather than templated corporate pitch-slaps. |
| **5** | **Integrated SDR-to-AE Handoff** | When a meeting is booked, generates a concise briefing with verified facts, likely priorities, unresolved gaps, discovery probes, and claims to avoid. | Handoff integrity; discovery calls convert into Stage 2 qualified pipeline. |
| **6** | **Baseline-Driven Value Proof** | Measures research time saved, evidence corrections, meetings held, and AE acceptance against a measured manual baseline. | Defensible ROI data required to justify recurring contract renewal. |

## 4. The 5-Dimension Prioritization Heuristic ($H_{\text{priority}}$)

Instead of an opaque, artificial composite score, the engine exposes five transparent dimensions:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    THE 5 TRANSPARENT SELECTION DIMENSIONS                   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                             │
│  1. ICP FIT (0-100)           ──► Is this company a viable customer for us? │
│  2. BUYING MOMENTUM (0-100)   ──► Is there a verified reason to act now?    │
│  3. EVIDENCE CONFIDENCE (%)   ──► How reliable and fresh is our data?       │
│  4. DEAL POTENTIAL (ACV)      ──► What contract value is realistic? (£15k+) │
│  5. PURSUIT COST (£)          ──► How much rep time/API cost will this take?│
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

$$H_{\text{priority}} = \Big( \frac{\text{ICP Fit}}{100} \times \frac{\text{Buying Momentum}}{100} \times \text{Estimated ACV} \times C_{\text{epistemic}} \Big) - C_{\text{pursuit}}$$

*Note*: This metric is explicitly a **capacity-allocation heuristic**, not an actuarial financial forecast. It ensures expensive selling time is concentrated on high-yield accounts with verified momentum.

## 5. Product Development Priority Order

We follow a strict, disciplined build sequence:

```
P0 (Core Trust & Decision):
  ├── Account Qualification: ICP criteria, deal floor, and hard exclusions.
  ├── Evidence Dossier: Primary citations, quotes, facts vs inferences, contradiction flags.
  └── Prioritization & Daily Queue: 10-20 accounts/day with explicit next actions.

P1 (Execution & Handoff):
  ├── Stakeholder Map: Organic buying-group mapping without synthetic guessing.
  ├── Account-Specific Pursuit Plan: Commercial hypothesis, opening question, <75w draft.
  └── Outcome Learning: Capture won/lost/reply data to calibrate future weights.

EXPLICITLY DEFERRED (Post-Validation):
  └── Autonomous sending, multi-agent swarms, full CRM replacement, elaborate dashboards.
```

## 6. Commercial Model: The Strategic Account Intelligence Sprint

We do not sell through unprovable guarantees. We sell three measurable business improvements:
1. **Sales Capacity**: Time required to research and prepare an account (aiming for $\ge 40\%$ reduction).
2. **Decision Quality**: Percentage of material claims supported by valid evidence ($\ge 95\%$).
3. **Pipeline Contribution**: Meetings held and AE-accepted opportunities versus baseline.

### The Offer:
* **Product**: Strategic Account Intelligence Sprint
* **Target**: Founder-led B2B SaaS (£15k–£100k+ ACV)
* **Test Price**: £1,000 – £2,000 flat
* **Duration**: 30 Days
* **Scope**: 1 ICP motion across an agreed batch of 30–50 named accounts
* **Deliverables**:
  1. Evidence-backed account dossiers and daily action queue.
  2. Stakeholder mapping and diagnostic pursuit plans.
  3. Pre-meeting discovery briefs for Account Executives.
  4. Final baseline-versus-pilot performance report.
