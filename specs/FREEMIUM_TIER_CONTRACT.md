# ⚖️ Freemium Tier Contract: VerneDaily Free Skill vs. Team Intelligence Platform

## 1. Overview & Strategic Positioning

**VerneDaily** (`www.vernedaily.xyz`) follows an evidence-led, bottom-up Product-Led Growth (PLG) distribution model:

- **Free Tier (VerneDaily SDR Free)**: Distributed freely as an open skill to individual SDRs and BDRs. Zero corporate IT approval required. Runs locally or via CLI/agent prompt. Delivers personal quota relief and high-conviction account research: outputs a shareable **Account Intelligence Brief & AE Pre-Meeting Memo** with verified citations, a diagnostic problem hypothesis, and progressive qualification gaps.
- **Paid Tier (VerneDaily Team Intelligence Platform)**: Delivered initially as a **30-Day Paid Team Pilot** ($1,500 – $3,000 / month per 5-rep pod) for VPs of Sales and RevOps. Unlocks collaborative account memory, multi-rep territory collision locking, dynamic stakeholder mapping, automated contact-policy governance, signal decay alerts, and progressive AE qualification dossiers.

## 4. Defensibility & Pilot Validation Gates

Before pitching annual enterprise subscriptions, the paid platform validates three commercial hypotheses:
1. **Intelligence Superiority**: Does the engine identify actionable operational signals that experienced SDRs miss?
2. **Held-Meeting Conversion**: Does progressive qualification with explicit unknowns increase the percentage of meetings that convert into Stage 2 pipeline?
3. **Willingness to Pay**: Will a sales leader renew after a 30-day pilot based on measurable pipeline creation rather than generic AI buzzwords?
