# ⚖️ Freemium Tier Contract: VerneDaily Free Skill vs. God Mode SaaS

## 1. Overview & Strategy

**VerneDaily** (`www.vernedaily.xyz`) follows an open-core, bottom-up Product-Led Growth (PLG) architecture inspired by the developer-first, evidence-led precision of TchaProtection (`tcha.dev`).

- **Free Tier (VerneDaily SDR Free)**: Distributed freely to individual SDRs/BDRs. Zero IT approval required. Runs locally or via CLI/agent prompt. Delivers personal quota relief: 75-word SPIN drafter, 15-second phone battlecard, and Chris Voss objection handlers.
- **Paid Tier (VerneDaily God Mode SaaS)**: Enterprise subscription for sales leaders, RevOps, and multi-rep teams. Unlocks two-way CRM sync (Salesforce/HubSpot), the 6.8-person committee state machine, autonomous calendar/holiday engine, collision locking, and BANT AE handoffs.
